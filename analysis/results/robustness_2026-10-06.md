## Definitions

- Bound (a) (headline of the census): a pair of entries with scores P1 >= P2 on a board of n tasks is *separable* when gap = P1 - P2 is not below 1.96 sqrt(SE1^2 + SE2^2 - 2 rho SE1 SE2), SE = sqrt(P(1-P)/n), and *not separable* otherwise; a tie (gap = 0) is always not separable. rho is the board's headline rho: the median over adjacent pairs of the phi correlation of per-task outcomes for the 7 boards with per-task data, the pooled median 0.5218 for every other board (`census_summary.md`).
- Adjacent pair: ranks (i, i+1) in the census order (score descending, ties by entry name). #1 vs #2 is the first adjacent pair; #1 vs #5 is the pair of ranks 1 and 5.

## 1. McNemar validation of the item-sampling bound

Test, alpha = 0.05, two-sided, on the entries' per-task outcomes. *Test separable* = p < 0.05. A single-run pair (both outcome vectors 0/1: the four SWE-bench boards and TheAgentCompany, and Terminal-Bench 2.0 pairs of two one-run entries) uses the exact McNemar test (exact binomial test of the discordant tasks, b = x solves and y fails vs c = the reverse, p = 0.5; p = 1 when there is no discordant task). A pair with an entry that averages k > 1 runs (Terminal-Bench 1.0 with k = 5; Terminal-Bench 2.0 with k up to 10) has per-task success shares in [0, 1], not outcomes; for those the **paired t-test on the per-task differences of the shares** is used (n - 1 degrees of freedom); the Wilcoxon signed-rank and exact sign p-values are in the pairs CSV. Agreement categories: *both separable* (bound and test both separate the pair), *both not separable*, *bound only* (the bound separates, the test does not), *test only* (the test separates, the bound does not). Agree = both separable + both not separable. The bound is evaluated on the census scores (for Terminal-Bench 2.0 the share of all trials solved, which differs slightly from the mean of the per-task shares when an entry has unequal trial counts per task); no multiplicity correction is applied to the pair tests.

## 2. Calibration simulation of bound (a)

Per task, a pair of Bernoulli outcomes with marginal success probabilities P1, P2 and phi correlation rho (joint cell probability P(1,1) = P1 P2 + rho sqrt(P1(1-P1)P2(1-P2))). An entry's score is the share of n tasks solved; each cell uses 20,000 independent replications and a fixed seed (20261006, one stream per cell). Rule: *separated* when |D| > 1.96 SE_D, D = P1_hat - P2_hat, SE_D^2 = SE1^2 + SE2^2 - 2 rho SE1 SE2 with SE_j = sqrt(P_j_hat(1 - P_j_hat)/n) from the OBSERVED scores (plug-in, as in the census) and rho fixed at the TRUE value. **False-separation rate**: share of replications that are separated when the true difference is 0 (P1 = P2 = P); the nominal value is 0.05; Monte Carlo SE of a rate near 0.05 is 0.0015. **Power at the bound**: share separated when the true difference equals the nominal bound delta = 1.96 sqrt(2 P(1-P)(1-rho)/n) (entries at P + delta/2 and P - delta/2); for a z-test whose critical value equals the true difference the power is 0.50.

Validation of the simulation: for n = 50 and n = 100 the exact false-separation probability (enumeration of every multinomial outcome) differs from the simulated rate by at most 0.0046 over the 12 (P, rho) cells at n = 50 and 0.0026 at n = 100 (consistent with the Monte Carlo SE above; differences of about 0.005 between cells are noise).

## 4. Terminal-Bench 2.0: trials with no verifier reward treated as missing

Baseline = the existing analysis: a trial with no verifier reward counts as a failure. Sensitivity = those trials are dropped (926 of 32,803 trials in the snapshot; 923 of them belong to the 72 analysis-set submissions) and a task's rate is K/N over the valid trials. The 72 submissions and 89 tasks of the existing analysis set are kept, because re-applying the rule of at least 5 trials per task after the drop leaves only 13 of the 72; after the drop 15 (submission, task) cells have no valid trial (that task is skipped for the submission), 39 have one, 460 have fewer than 5 (of 6,408 cells). A cell with one valid trial has no resampling variance, so run-to-run SDs of the sensitivity are, if anything, understated. Missing-aware task-sampling and split-half versions are used (`analysis/robustness_tb2.py` docstring); on complete data they reproduce the existing ones. B = 10,000 bootstrap replicates (the full number; the run took about 20 s, so no reduction was needed), 1,000 split-half splits, the seed scheme of `bootstrap_core`. ## 5. Multiplicity: Bonferroni and Holm versions of the headline

The census headline (bound (a), empirical rho, the 22 analysed boards) with the critical value 1.96 replaced by an adjusted one. (i) Bonferroni over the m - 1 adjacent pairs of a board (m = entries): z = Phi^-1(1 - 0.025 / (m - 1)); this z is used for the adjacent share, the tiers and the all-pairs share. (ii) Bonferroni over the 2 top-entry comparisons (#1 vs #2, #1 vs #5) of a board: z = Phi^-1(1 - 0.0125) = 2.2414. (iii) Holm over all m(m-1)/2 pairs of a board at familywise alpha = 0.05: each pair's two-sided normal p-value of gap / SE_D (SE_D = bound (a) at z = 1, rho fixed at the board's rho; gap = 0 gives p = 1), step-down thresholds alpha / (M - k + 1); the share reported is the share of pairs not rejected. Everything else is as in `census_summary.md`. Per-board values: `robustness_2026-10-06_multiplicity_boards.csv`.

## 8. Calibration of the Terminal-Bench 2.0 run-to-run bootstrap (Eq. 6)

Truth: the per-task probabilities p_t are the observed per-task success shares of a real submission (the 72 analysis-set submissions of `tb2_long.csv`; 1 of them, MAYA__Claude-4.5-sonnet, has every task share at 0 or 1, so its true SD is 0 and it is left out of the ratios and the coverage summaries). Each simulated dataset has r runs per task, Bernoulli(p_t), for r = 5 and r = 10. The corrected bootstrap (p*_t = phat_t + sqrt(r/(r-1)) (ptilde_t - phat_t), B = 2,000 replicates) is applied to every simulated dataset. 1,000 simulated datasets per submission and setting for the 10 representative submissions (evenly spaced ranks over the score range) and for each of the 71 non-degenerate submissions (1,000 datasets each). Fixed seed 20261007. The bootstrap sum over mixed tasks is drawn as a sum of Binomial(m_k r, k/r) over the counts k of successes, which has exactly the distribution of `bootstrap_core.run_to_run` (checked in the tests) with fresh random streams. An uncorrected bootstrap (scale 1) is evaluated on the same draws for comparison. Runtime of the calibration: about 6 minutes at these settings, so no reduction was needed (B = 2,000 as requested). Files: `robustness_2026-10-06_tb2_calibration_submissions.csv`, `robustness_2026-10-06_tb2_calibration_pairs.csv`, `robustness_2026-10-06_tb2_calibration_flip_table.csv`, `robustness_2026-10-06_tb2_calibration_bins.csv`.

### (c) Flip-probability calibration

Pairs: each representative submission A and its shifted copy B with p_B = p_A (1 - g / P_A), so A is better by exactly g = 0.5, 1, 2, 3 pp (40 pairs per r). Per simulated dataset the observed order is the observed one (a tie goes to A) and the bootstrap flip probability is the share of the B bootstrap replicates (paired with the A replicates) in which the observed lower-ranked entry scores above the higher-ranked one, ties counted half. TRUE flip probability = probability that an independent rerun (fresh r runs per task for both entries) reverses the observed order, ties half, averaged over datasets: (pi+ + pi0)(pi- + pi0/2) + pi-(pi+ + pi0/2) with pi+, pi0, pi- the exact probabilities that the success-count difference of the two entries is positive, zero, negative (convolution, no simulation).
